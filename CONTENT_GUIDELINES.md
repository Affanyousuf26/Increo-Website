# Content guidelines

All public company, product, and performance claims require approval from Increo.

Do not publish invented customers, government relationships, deployments, certifications, patents, awards, manufacturing claims, partnerships, technical specifications, or readiness levels. Describe technology areas as research and engineering focus unless a stronger statement is approved.

The homepage Resilience Stack and product line are sourced directly from the supplied Increo Pitch Deck. Do not add layers, products, specifications, or capability claims that are absent from an approved company source. The generated hero UAS image is conceptual and must not be described as operational hardware. Replace it with approved photography or CAD renders when available.

Before launch, approve the company positioning copy, official logo, business contact details, canonical domain, social links, privacy language, and product naming.
